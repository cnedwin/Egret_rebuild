## Numerical and authority boundary

The contract uses existing ordered parent × local binary64 expressions. Authored rectangles with either extent zero consume primitive budget and skip endpoint/corner arithmetic; descendant matrix checks still apply. Positive content contributes evaluated direct corners even after finite rounding or underflow collapses geometry. Nonfinite admitted matrices/endpoints/corners/final extents reject with `BOUNDS_ARITHMETIC_RANGE`.

Extent subtraction is ordinary binary64 and provides no outward-rounded conservative enclosure. The literal extrema 1 and 2^53+2 yield width 2^53 after nearest-even rounding. A future hit-test/broad-phase rejecting bound needs its own numerical contract. Unknown option fields are ignored without reads; exact supplied throw causes and private receiver/lifetime authority retain their declared order.
