The sequence consumer uses an owned opaque 6×2 image, two genuine independent leases of one generation, Bitmap, SequencePlayer and its Scope-owned subscription. Durations are 0.125/0.25/0.125; crops are 2×2, 1×2 and 3×1. Game time 0.125 selects frame 1; separate `applyAt(0.375)` and backward `applyAt(0)` remain valid. Capture does not tick or acquire again. No fake public counter exhaustion, private-state injection, physical heap/GC, callback preemption or throughput claim is used.
