## Advancing choices

Separate questions into mechanism facts, Egret implementation and adaptation, and product outcomes. Pinned source and official specifications support mechanism judgments. Implementation and adaptation require corresponding regressions; performance, energy use, package size, and host compatibility require the actual execution environment. Continue research and reversible implementation supported by the current environment, while leaving device-dependent conclusions pending measurement.

| Situation | Action | Resulting design and validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard mechanisms and mature implementations meet Egret's goals | Pin versions and the scope read, extract mechanisms and error paths, and identify public dependencies and independent modules | Use established principles; validate independent modules against their specifications |
| Interfaces, hosts, or resource models differ | Evaluate integration costs through public interfaces and establish an Egret adaptation contract or independent design | Validate boundary handoff, lifecycle, migration, and failure paths |
| Alternatives have different costs and quantitative conditions are unavailable | Select a replaceable research baseline and record alternatives and switching conditions | Advance reversible implementation and add comparisons in the corresponding environment |
| Upstream maintenance exposes defects or semantic gaps | Check issues, merged PRs, and affected versions; track dependency fixes and add matching regressions for independent modules | Retain concrete counterexamples and applicable versions without extrapolating to overall project quality |
| The question concerns real-device behavior | Register a minimal device matrix, inputs, measurement methods, and unmeasured items | Complete platform-independent contracts first; determine support from device results |
| The area differentiates Egret or lacks a usable solution | Conduct necessary new design and prototype research, recording alternatives and failing inputs | Choose what to retain using correctness, product-task, and cost evidence |

Record adoption uniformly as mechanism research, research runtime dependency, explicit third-party dependency, or independent implementation. Reading material creates a research basis; integration and architecture decisions retain their own statuses. Foundational mechanism research and reversible adaptation may continue within existing requirements. Specific product choices are determined by corresponding technical decisions and acceptance records.

## What each reference must retain

Record the official repository or documentation URL, tag and full commit, files or interfaces, scope read and executed, hashes of acquired files, maintenance date, license indicators, applicable mechanisms, differences from Egret contracts, and integration regressions and replacement conditions. Use the main branch to discover paths; pin snapshots when establishing versioned design evidence. Do not combine different versions into an unexplained whole.

Prioritize critical call chains and error paths. For caches, inspect keys, invalidation, retention, and destruction; for recovery, inspect data sources, asynchronous cancellation, and responsibility for external resources; for text, inspect actual measurement, drawing, and upload. READMEs, vendor performance figures, and popularity provide context, not substitutes for mechanism evidence.

For proposed dependencies, inspect declarations for the actual files, fonts, resources, transcoders, and downstream dependencies separately. Keep dependency identities distinct, and record upstream baselines and local patches. Independent implementations must record Egret specifications, design tradeoffs, and research grounds and undergo provenance review using the [implementation provenance template](../templates/实现来源记录.en.md).

## Research, development, and validation responsibilities

| Work | Main output | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research into mature mechanisms | Pinned-version summaries, maintenance counterexamples, interface and semantic comparisons | Source and documentation establish only the scope read; unexecuted content remains unexecuted |
| Independent core implementation | Specification, code, regressions, provenance, and design explanations | Passing behavior is limited to the corresponding slice, not an entire product pass |
| Third-party integration | Dependency inventory, adapter code, notices, integration regressions | Attribute dependency capabilities and Egret adaptation capabilities separately |
| Product and device validation | Comparable task samples, functional/visual comparisons, device and host results | Actual environment results determine support and benefits |

Egret focuses investment on complex UI and 3D composition, resource lifecycles under limited memory, lightweight builds, engineering contracts for sustained AI editing, legacy semantic migration, and target-platform experience. General infrastructure dependencies are integrated through Egret interfaces. Egret centrally organizes project originals, stable identity, diagnostics, and undo.
