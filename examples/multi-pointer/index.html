Two independent regions / 两个独立区域

Hold one finger in each colored region, then move either finger outside the canvas. Each contact keeps its own target until it ends or is cancelled.
分别按住两个色块，再将任意触点移出画布。每个触点在结束或取消之前保留自己的目标。

Starting / 正在启动

    Mouse, touch and pen use Pointer Events. Resizing, scrolling, hiding the page or losing focus cancels held contacts. Only the canvas disables touch scrolling.
    鼠标、触摸和笔统一使用 Pointer Events。尺寸变化、滚动、页面隐藏或失焦会取消按住的触点。只有画布禁用触摸滚动。
