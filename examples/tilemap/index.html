A tile map / 瓦片地图

A sect courtyard of 64 by 40 cells, made in Tiled and read from its JSON. The arrow keys or WASD walk the disciple; the camera follows, and only the chunks in view are drawn. Click a cell of grass to plant flowers or take them away.
一个 64×40 格的门派庭院，用 Tiled 制作并从其 JSON 读入。方向键或 WASD 让弟子行走；相机跟随，只绘制视野内的区块。点击草地上的一格可以种花或拔掉。
