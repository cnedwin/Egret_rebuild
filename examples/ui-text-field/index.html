Text fields / 文本框控件

A name, a motto and a button, drawn by the engine. Press a field and type, with an input method if you use one. Or click the canvas and use the keyboard: Tab moves, Enter opens a field or presses the button. In the name, Enter commits; in the motto, press elsewhere. Escape cancels.
名称、宣言和一个按钮，全部由引擎绘制。点击输入框即可输入，可以使用输入法。也可以先点一下画布再用键盘：Tab 移动，回车打开输入框或按下按钮。名称用回车提交，宣言点击别处提交，Esc 取消。
