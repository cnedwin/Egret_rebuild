List semantics / 列表语义

A list of fifty thousand servers, drawn by the engine. Eight rows show, and only those are realized. Press a row to choose it, or click the canvas and use the keyboard: Tab enters the list, the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End move through it, and Space or Enter chooses the row with the focus. Arrow Right opens the details of a row in a modal card. Assistive technology is given a list box whose options say where they stand among the fifty thousand.
一个包含五万台服务器的列表，由引擎绘制。可见的只有八行，也只有这八行被实例化。按下某一行即可选中它；也可以点一下画布后使用键盘：Tab 进入列表，方向键、Page Up、Page Down、Home 和 End 在其中移动，空格或回车选中获得焦点的行。右方向键在一张模态卡片中打开该行的详情。辅助技术得到的是一个列表框，其中每个选项都说明自己在五万项中的位置。
