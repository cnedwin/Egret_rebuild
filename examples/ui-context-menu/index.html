Menu of a row / 行的操作菜单

A roster of forty members. Press a member to select it. Its menu opens with the right mouse button, with the Menu key or Shift+F10 on the focused row, or with the Actions button. The arrow keys move among the items, Enter does one, and Escape or a press elsewhere closes the menu.
四十名成员的名册。按下某个成员即可选中。用鼠标右键、在获得焦点的行上按菜单键或 Shift+F10，或者按“操作”按钮，可以打开它的菜单。方向键在菜单项之间移动，回车执行，按 Escape 或按在别处会关闭菜单。
