Several of a list / 列表多选

A roster of forty members. Press members to choose several, or move with the arrow keys and press Space; then send them out. A member who is away cannot be chosen. The buttons choose everyone, clear the choice, and send.
四十名成员的名册。按下成员可以选中多个，也可以用方向键移动再按空格；然后把他们派出去。已经外出的成员不能再选。几个按钮分别是全选、清除和派遣。
