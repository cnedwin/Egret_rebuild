Settings screen / 设置界面

One screen drawn by the engine: a switch, a group of options, a text field, a theme switch, a volume slider, a button that asks before it resets, and a list of two hundred profiles. Use a mouse, a finger, or the keyboard: Tab moves from control to control and treats the list as one stop, Space or Enter activates, and the arrow keys move within the group and the list and move the slider. The name takes Chinese through the input method of the platform.
