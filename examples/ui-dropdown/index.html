Dropdown in a list / 列表中的下拉选择

A roster of forty members, each with a job. Press a member, or move to one with the arrow keys and press Enter, and a list of jobs opens under that row, outside the clip of the roster. Choose with a press or with the arrow keys and Enter; Escape or a press elsewhere closes it and the focus returns to the row. The wheel scrolls the roster, and the list of jobs follows its row until the row is gone.
四十名成员的名册，每人有一项职务。按下某个成员，或用方向键移到他并按回车，职务列表就在这一行下面打开，不受名册裁剪的限制。用按下或方向键加回车选择；按 Escape 或按在别处会关闭它，焦点回到这一行。滚轮滚动名册，职务列表跟随它所在的行，直到这一行消失。
