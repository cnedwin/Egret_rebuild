Tabs and progress / 标签页与进度

Three tabs drawn by the engine, each with its panel. Press a tab, or move to the tabs with Tab and use the arrow keys: the panel follows the selection. The second panel has three progress bars and a button that advances them.
由引擎绘制的三个标签页，各有自己的面板。按下某个标签，或用 Tab 移到标签上再用方向键：面板跟随选择切换。第二个面板里有三条进度条和一个推进它们的按钮。
