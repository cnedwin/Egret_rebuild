Amounts in steps / 按步分配数量

A hundred spirit stones to share among four halls. Press − or +, or move to an amount with Tab and use the Up and Down arrows, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End. The line under the halls says how many are left, and says so when an amount cannot go up.
一百枚灵石分给四个堂口。按 − 或 +，或者用 Tab 移到某个数量上，再用上下方向键、Page Up、Page Down、Home 和 End。堂口下面的一行说明还剩多少；某个数量无法再增加时也会说明原因。
