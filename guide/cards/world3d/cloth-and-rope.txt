# Cloth and rope in 3D: whole edges, slack as a share, culling, one-way pins

Tea Horse Road ties tarps and lashings on its trucks, hangs prayer flags between poles and slings a bridge on cables. Four things went wrong first, each silent:

```js
import { createCloth3D, createRope3D } from '@egret/engine/world3d';

// right and down are the WHOLE edges of the cloth, not the gap between two points.
const tarp = createCloth3D({ columns: 9, rows: 6, origin: [-1.2, 1.1, -0.2], right: [2.3, 0, 0], down: [0, 0, 0.8] });
for (let c = 0; c < tarp.columns; c++) tarp.pin(c, 0, body); // follows the truck body, does not pull it

// slack is the extra share of length: 0.04 is 4 % longer than the straight line, not 1.04.
const cable = createRope3D({ start: from, end: to, segments: 40, slack: 0.04, pinStart: true, pinEnd: true });

const mesh = tarp.createMesh(lashing);
mesh.frustumCulled = false; // its bounds stay where it was made (engine 687037a)
```

Rules:

- `right` and `down` span the whole cloth. Passed as the spacing, a tarp and every flag shrank to a knot.
- `slack` is the share added to the straight length. Passed as a factor (1.02), the bridge cables folded under the deck.
- At `687037a` a cloth or rope mesh keeps the bounding sphere of where it was made, so once it travels with a vehicle it is frustum-culled and vanishes some tens of metres on. Set `frustumCulled = false` on meshes that move far; ones that only sway in place can keep culling.
- `pin` and `pinPoint` with a body make points follow it; they do not push or drag the body back.
- Call `update(seconds)` once a frame for each, after the vehicle has moved, and keep the iterations low (6–16): they are paid every frame on the main thread.
