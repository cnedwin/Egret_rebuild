# A role-playing game: a world to walk, a party, fights and a story

**Loop.** Walk the world, talk, take quests, fight, grow stronger, go on; fights are turn-based on a grid (Heroes of Jin Yong's kind) or in real time (Fengshen Chronicle).

**Files.** Maps, people, items, skills and quests as data; the campaign, the battle and progression as rules tested in Node (`src/sim/campaign.mjs`, `src/sim/progression.mjs`); dialogue apart from what it shows; saves in slots.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/web/game` for a 2D world and battles; `@egret/engine/world3d` with `@egret/engine/webgl2/world3d` for a 3D one; gamepad and keyboard entries.

**Read first.** `save-data`, `game-screens`, `balance-from-runs`, `fast-startup`, `asset-loading`, `pause-and-overlays`.

**Done means.** The main quest finished by a script in Node; every fight winnable by the party the story gives; three saves that load in a new window; menus by keys, a pad and taps. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from JY and Fengshen Chronicle.
