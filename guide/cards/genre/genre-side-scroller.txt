# A side-scrolling action game: run, jump, combos and bosses

**Loop.** Move and jump through an act, fight with combos, reach and beat its boss; each act adds a move.

**Files.** The hero, foes, bosses and acts as rules (`src/rules/`) stepped at a fixed rate and tested in Node; levels as tile data; the view draws puppets or sprites from what the rules say each frame.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/web/game` with `@egret/engine/camera`, `@egret/engine/tilemap` and `@egret/engine/particles`; `@egret/spine` for skeletal puppets; `@egret/engine/web/gamepad`.

**Read first.** `action-body-feedback`, `touch-controls`, `keyboard-shortcuts`, `sequence-markers`, `two-colour-tints`, `asset-provenance`.

**Done means.** The first jump within a second of Start; coyote time and a jump buffer set in the rules and tested; every boss beaten by a scripted input in Node; a pad, keys and buttons on the glass. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Lantern Shadows.
