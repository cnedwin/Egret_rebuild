# A tactics or strategy game on a board or a map

**Loop.** Choose units or armies, move and fight on a board or a map, end the turn or let time run; the other side does the same by an AI of the same rules.

**Files.** Maps, units and factions as data; moves, combat, fog and the AI as rules (`src/sim/rules.mjs`, `src/sim/ai.mjs`, `src/sim/fog.mjs`), tested by playing AI against AI; the board drawn from the state.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/world3d` with `@egret/engine/webgpu/world3d` or `@egret/engine/webgl2/world3d`; a page canvas or atlas labels for many names.

**Read first.** `tile-board-camera`, `toon-figure-hooks`, `label-pages`, `camera-page-layer`, `terrain-stream-tiles`, `world3d-draw-cost`.

**Done means.** A whole campaign or scenario played AI against AI without a stuck turn; every unit chosen by keys, a pad and a tap; the arrows following the view; the largest map measured. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Starbanner Tactics and Nine Provinces Ablaze.
