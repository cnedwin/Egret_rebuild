# A rhythm game: a chart, a clock and judging strokes

**Loop.** Notes travel to a line in time with the music; the player strikes; each stroke is judged early, on time or late; a song ends in a grade.

**Files.** Charts as data (`src/sim/songs.mjs`, `src/sim/chart.mjs`); judging as rules with windows in milliseconds (`src/sim/judge.mjs`), tested with strokes at known times; a bot that strikes with a given spread (`src/sim/bot.mjs`), to show that a steady player clears each chart and random striking does not; the music rendered once and played as one voice.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/play` for the stage; `@egret/engine/web/audio` for the music voice whose `position` is the clock.

**Read first.** `rhythm-clock`, `moving-shapes`, `synthesized-audio`, `keyboard-shortcuts`.

**Done means.** The clock taken from the music, never from frames; strokes judged from their event time; a delay setting the player measures; every chart cleared by the steady bot and failed by the random one in Node; notes drawn from rectangles or measured. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.
