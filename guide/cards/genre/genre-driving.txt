# A driving or racing game: a vehicle, a track or a road, and rivals

**Loop.** Drive a vehicle along a track or a road, against rivals or the road itself, through checkpoints, to a finish.

**Files.** The vehicle's handling, the track or road and the rivals as rules stepped at a fixed rate (`src/sim.mjs`, `src/track.mjs`, `src/play/convoy.mjs`), tested by laps driven by the AI in Node; scenery placed from data and drawn by distance.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/world3d` with a backend entry; terrain streams, instanced scenery, cloth and rope where the road needs them.

**Read first.** `terrain-stream-tiles`, `cloth-and-rope`, `world3d-draw-cost`, `prepare-programs`, `touch-controls`, `action-body-feedback`.

**Done means.** Every rival finishes every track without a stall; a lap driven by keys, a pad and buttons on the glass (the kart may accelerate by itself on touch); the busiest stretch of road measured. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Jinxiu Rally and Tea Horse Road.
