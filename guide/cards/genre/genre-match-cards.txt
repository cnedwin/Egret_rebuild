# A match-three board with a card battle

**Loop.** Swap two neighbouring tiles; lines of three or more clear, fall and chain; what clears charges the other half of the game, here energy and cards for a turn-based battle.

**Files.** `src/core/board.mjs` (the grid, swaps, matches, gravity, refills from a seeded generator), `src/core/cards.mjs`, `src/core/battle.mjs` and `src/core/rng.mjs` are rules only, each tested on its own; the view animates what the rules report, step by step.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/web/game` with sprites and tweens; portraits from Spine where characters act.

**Read first.** `sim-view-split`, `gestures` (swap by drag or by two taps), `game-screens`, `two-colour-tints`, `synthesized-audio`.

**Done means.** No board starts with a match or without a move, which tests prove over many seeds; a swap by drag, by two taps and by keys; a whole encounter played by the rules in Node. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.
