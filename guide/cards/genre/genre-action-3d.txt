# A 3D action game: melee, shooting or duels

**Loop.** Move, attack, guard or dodge, read the enemy's tells; an encounter ends in a win or a fall; levels or a series of fights.

**Files.** Fighters, weapons and enemies as data; combat, AI and tells as rules stepped at a fixed rate and tested in Node (`src/sim/fight.mjs`, `src/rules/game.mjs`, `src/sim.mjs`); the view drives models from the rules' state; quality levels the player can choose.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/world3d` with `@egret/engine/webgpu/world3d` or `@egret/engine/webgl2/world3d`; `@egret/engine/input/gamepad`.

**Read first.** `action-body-feedback`, `prepare-programs`, `world3d-draw-cost`, `world3d-lights-shadows`, `model-in-code`, `asset-loading`, `touch-controls`.

**Done means.** A hit felt in the body and in sound within the frame it lands; programs prepared while loading, so the first fight does not stall; every encounter won by a scripted bot in Node; keys, a pad and the glass. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Five Regiments, Snow at the Mountain Temple and Operation Cutoff.
