# A survivors-like: a horde, weapons that fire by themselves, an upgrade each level

**Loop.** The player only moves; weapons fire by themselves; enemies come in growing waves; each level offers a choice of upgrades; a run lasts minutes and ends in a win or a fall.

**Files.** `src/sim/data.mjs` holds enemies, weapons and their levels; `src/sim/world.mjs` steps the horde, the shots and the pick-ups, capped in the rules; `src/sim/bot.mjs` plays runs for pacing. The view keeps every kind of sprite in a pool.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/play` in 2D (Nezha Stirs the Sea, with a stick of its own on the glass); `@egret/engine/world3d` in 3D (Jelly Pandas).

**Read first.** `sprite-swarm`, `moving-shapes`, `touch-controls`, `balance-from-runs`, `action-body-feedback`; in 3D `world3d-draw-cost` and `prepare-programs`.

**Done means.** Moving from the first second; an upgrade choice taken by keys, a tap and a gamepad; the cap on enemies set in the rules and the busiest moment measured; a whole run played by the bot in Node. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Nezha Stirs the Sea and Jelly Pandas.
