# A tabletop game against computer players: cards or tiles, turns and scoring

**Loop.** Deal, take turns, call or pass, win or draw, score; the computer players take their turns by the same rules.

**Files.** The deal, the turn and the calls as rules (`src/sim/game.mjs`), hands and scoring apart (`src/sim/hand.mjs`, `src/sim/score.mjs`), the computer players in `src/sim/bot.mjs`; thousands of hands played in Node test the rules and the bots.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/play`; a table laid out by the shape of the window.

**Read first.** `play-turned-table`, `play-resolution`, `sprite-swarm` (many tiles), `still-background` (the felt), `keyboard-shortcuts`, `two-languages`.

**Done means.** Every scoring rule tested on hands written by hand; many hands played by four bots without a stuck turn; every call by a key and a button; an auto-play switch. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Taiwan Sixteen-Tile Mahjong.
