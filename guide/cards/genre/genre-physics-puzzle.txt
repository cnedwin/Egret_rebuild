# A physics or falling-sand puzzle

**Loop.** The player aims or places, the world settles by its own rules, and the level is won when it reaches a state: a gate opened, the sand sorted.

**Files.** Levels as data (`src/levels/designs.mjs`); the predicted path or the sand step as rules tested in Node (`src/play/trajectory.mjs`, `src/sim/sand.mjs`); the view draws the settled world and replays what moved.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/physics2d` for bodies and joints, with `@egret/engine/camera`, `@egret/engine/tilemap` and `@egret/engine/particles` (Mechanism City); a grid stepped in the rules and drawn as textures (Sand Scroll).

**Read first.** `rope-joints-2d`, `pixel-strips`, `geometry-budget-2d`, `gestures`, `balance-from-runs`.

**Done means.** Every level solvable, proved by a solution played in Node; a reset that restores the level exactly; aiming by drag, keys and gamepad; the frame measured with the most bodies or the most sand moving. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Mechanism City and Sand Scroll.
