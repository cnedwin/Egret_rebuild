# A deduction game: evidence, notes and a verdict

**Loop.** Read the case, examine evidence, take words from it into notes, fill the blanks of a verdict; a wrong verdict says which part does not hold.

**Files.** Each case is data (`src/sim/cases/c1.mjs`…) checked by rules (`src/sim/rules.mjs`) that tests run over every case; an audit (`src/sim/audit.mjs`) proves each case has one answer reachable from its evidence. The view lays out long text, evidence cards and blanks.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/play` for the frame; `@egret/engine/paragraph` (with `@egret/engine/web/paragraph`) for long, selectable text; `@egret/engine/web/text-input` and editing for notes; semantics for keys and screen readers.

**Read first.** `label-pages`, `label-wrap`, `touch-labels`, `two-languages`, `game-screens`; for a mini game `ship-wechat-minigame`.

**Done means.** Every case solved by its audit in Node, and every wrong verdict told why; the whole game played by keys alone; text that fits at 390 pixels wide in both languages. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.
