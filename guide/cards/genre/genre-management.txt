# A management or city-building game: build, produce, wait, grow

**Loop.** Place buildings, produce and trade, wait while the world runs, unlock more; a long game checked in short visits.

**Files.** Buildings, goods and unlocks as data (`src/sim/data.mjs`); the economy as rules stepped by time (`src/sim/world.mjs`); a bot that plays a simulated player's day to tune the pacing (Peach Creek's `tools/pacing.mjs`); time away worked out when the game is opened again.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/play` with `@egret/engine/ui` for sheets and lists (Peach Creek); `@egret/engine/world3d` for a 3D seat (Wulin).

**Read first.** `balance-from-runs`, `save-data`, `game-screens`, `ui-callbacks`, `touch-labels`, `pause-and-overlays`.

**Done means.** The first building placed within ten seconds; pacing tuned from bot runs, with the numbers written down; time away applied once and capped; every sheet usable by keys and a screen reader. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Magistrate of Peach Creek and Wulin.
