# A story and life sim: days, places, people and choices

**Loop.** A calendar of days split into parts; each part spent at a place does one thing; people's affection, stats and money change; events and endings follow from them.

**Files.** People, places, items and events as data (`src/data/`); the day and its effects as rules (`src/sim.mjs`), tested by playing whole calendars; the story reader apart from what it reads; a save after every action.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine` display objects with the web input entries (pointer, keyboard, hover) and semantics; paragraphs when the text is long.

**Read first.** `game-screens`, `save-data`, `two-languages`, `label-wrap`, `pause-and-overlays`, `touch-labels`.

**Done means.** Every ending reached by a scripted calendar in Node; no event that can never fire; a save after every action, resumed in a new window; all choices by keys, taps and a screen reader. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.
