# A tower defence: waves along a path, towers on slots

**Loop.** Enemies come along a path in waves; the player spends what kills earn on towers in fixed slots and on their upgrades; a wave that gets through costs lives. A speed switch (×1, ×2, ×3) and a pause belong in the first version.

**Files.** `src/sim/data.mjs` holds towers, enemies and waves as plain data; `src/sim/world.mjs` steps the state and gives events (shot, hit, leaked, wave over); `src/sim/bot.mjs` plays whole runs in Node to tune the waves. `src/view.mjs` draws the state; `src/saves.mjs` keeps progress.

**Entries.** `@egret/engine/play` carries a page game (Canal Watch). A phone game held upright with many screens uses `@egret/engine/web/game` with `@egret/engine/ui` (Night Wardens).

**Read first.** `sim-view-split`, `balance-from-runs`, `sprite-swarm` (many moving enemies), `still-background` (the map), `play-page-controls` (build slots as page buttons), `sequence-markers` (walk cycles), `two-colour-tints` (hit flashes).

**Done means.** The first wave within five seconds of Start; a tower placed by a tap and by keys; every wave beaten by the bot in Node at each difficulty; the busiest wave measured, not guessed; a run resumed from its save. `npm test` plays the rules; `node vendor/egret.mjs check` gives no error; `node vendor/egret.mjs verify` passes with its pictures looked at.

Learnt from Canal Watch and Night Wardens.
