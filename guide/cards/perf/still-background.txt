# Paint a still background once, under a clear play canvas

A sprite that never changes is still drawn every frame. Large translucent fills (washes of hills, a wide river drawn twice for its banks) cost fill, not triangles. In Canal Watch at engine `b5628eb`, one such map sprite made the title's frame time 33.3 ms p95 in headless Chromium with SwiftShader at 1280 × 800; hidden, it was 16.7 ms. At engine `2e53f45`, a 1280 × 720 sprite of six translucent hill washes and two translucent river bands ran at 50 ms p50; hidden, 16.7 ms. `play` has no way to bake a sprite into one texture.

Measure first: hide the sprite (`sprite.visible = false`) and compare p95 frame times. If it is the background, let the page paint it once:

```html
<div id="field" style="position:relative;aspect-ratio:16/9">
  <canvas id="ground" style="position:absolute;inset:0;width:100%;height:100%"></canvas>
</div>
```

```js
const game = await play({ width: 960, height: 540, parent: field, transparent: true, setup, update });
paintGround(document.querySelector('#ground'));             // 2D canvas, repainted only on resize
```

Keep one drawing for both sides: write the background as `drawMap(g)` with Graphics call names (`beginFill`, `lineStyle`, `moveTo`, `lineTo`, `curveTo`, `drawRect`, `drawCircle`) and give it a small adapter that turns those calls into Canvas 2D, scaled from stage units to the box's pixels.

Rules:

- Only what never moves goes to the page. Towers, raiders, shots, gates and anything that changes stay in `play`.
- Repaint the 2D canvas from a `ResizeObserver` at `devicePixelRatio` (2 at most), not every frame.
- Measure again in the crowded part of the game, not only the title: Canal Watch was 16.8 ms p95 in its tenth wave afterwards.

Learnt from Canal Watch (ENGINE-NOTES 3).
