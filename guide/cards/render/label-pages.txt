# Many words on screen: atlas labels share pages; glyph sheets for banners

A frame takes at most 64 images and 4,096 regions. Each `browser.createLabel` of `createBrowserGame` is an image of its own, so at engine `9d801be` a map with a hundred place names failed its first frame with `FRAME_IMAGE_BUDGET`.

- **Many short labels** (place names, unit counts, a score board): `browser.createAtlasLabel` takes the same options and draws on pages it shares with other such labels. In `examples/label-budget` a hundred labels on one screen take one page, and once drawn they hold nothing in the text cache. `game.text` of `play` is drawn this way.
- **Words on things that move** from a fixed set of characters (banners, unit flags): draw every character once into one canvas, lease it once with `textureFromCanvas`, and cut each one out with `sourceRect`.
- **Still words under the action**: paint them on a page canvas under a clear engine canvas (see camera-page-layer).

```js
const names = await Promise.all(places.map(place => browser.createAtlasLabel({ text: place.name, fontSize: 18, color: 0xf4ead2 })));
names.forEach((label, i) => Object.assign(map.addChild(label.display), { x: places[i].x, y: places[i].y }));

// A banner from a glyph sheet made once at start: at[ch] is the cell of each character on the sheet.
const lease = await browser.textureFromCanvas(sheet);
const flag = banner.addChild(scope.use(new Bitmap(lease)));
flag.sourceRect = at['曹'];
```

Rules:

- Count images before choosing: one per `browser.createLabel`, a few shared pages for every `browser.createAtlasLabel`, 64 a frame. `browser.getFrameUsage()` says what the last frame held, and `onWarning` is told `FRAME_IMAGES_NEAR_LIMIT` before a frame is refused.
- A `textureFromCanvas` lease belongs to the game's scope and ends with the game: make one sheet with every character the game can need and share it, not one per battle.
- Paint glyphs white and tint by what is behind them, so one sheet serves every colour.

Learnt from Nine Provinces Ablaze (ENGINE-NOTES 1 and 3): 121 place names went to a page canvas and every banner reads from one sheet of about 250 characters. Shared pages came in engine `4de55e8` (#476).
