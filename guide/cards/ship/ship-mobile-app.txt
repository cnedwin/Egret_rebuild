# A complete phone game shipped as an app in a store

An app is installed on purpose and played for weeks, many minutes at a time. It is judged on depth, polish and how it behaves as an app, not on its first seconds alone.

- **Hosts.** Android has the project player: an arm64 app or a view in an existing Activity, drawing through OpenGL ES 3, with the installed System WebView (Android 8 and later) or V8 in the app (Android 10 and later). Its game is written to `installPlayerProject` with the engine and a scope, not to `play`: keep the rules apart from the picture (`sim-view-split`) so both builds share them. The initial iOS host runs a bundled 2D EGR2 quad scene with JavaScriptCore and Metal. Check the [Apple build and device guide](../../../runtime-sdk/ios/README.md) before choosing it for a game. Native input, audio, advanced draw formats, World3D, code signing and physical iPhone acceptance need their own work.
- **A phone in the hand.** Touch is the only input: thumbs on both sides, nothing under a finger that hides what it does, targets large enough to press (`touch-controls`, `touch-labels`). Lay out for notches and safe areas, held upright or sideways (`mobile-viewport`).
- **An app's life.** The game pauses when sent to the background and resumes from where it was; Android's back button opens a pause or leaves a menu, it does not quit mid-level. Forward the Activity's pause and resume to the player view.
- **Saves that last.** Progress goes to the app's own files through `services.storage` and `createSaveStore`, versioned so an update keeps it (`save-data`). Plan for a new phone: an account or a cloud copy.
- **Hours, not minutes.** Settings for sound, music and quality; a battery and heat budget, measured over a long session on a mid-range phone; content and progression for return visits (`balance-from-runs`).
- **The store.** Signing, a privacy policy and data declaration, an age rating, screenshots in each required size. Payments and ads come from the store's own SDKs, which the engine does not supply.

Before shipping: install the release build cold on several real phones, play a long session, kill and reopen it mid-level, and update over an old save.
