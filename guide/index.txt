# Egret guide: cards on demand

Cards are in English and name their sources; ask in either language.

- Ask: `node tools/egret-guide.mjs <words | API name | ERROR_CODE | hint-id>` prints the best two or three cards within `--budget` tokens (2,500).
- One card: `node tools/egret-guide.mjs --card <id>`. This list: `--list`.
- All cards in one file: `node tools/egret-guide.mjs --full --out <file>`.

`applies` identifies the engine entry or host. Stale cards explain what changed.

## How cards are made

Cards come from games built on the engine; `tools/harvest-games.mjs` proposes them and `tests/guide.test.mjs` checks them ([the loop](../knowledge-base/en/docs/agent-guide.md)).
