# Egret guide: cards on demand

Cards are in English and name their sources; ask in either language. A question costs fewer tokens than a list.

- Ask: `node tools/egret-guide.mjs <words | API name | ERROR_CODE | hint-id>` prints the best cards within `--budget` tokens (2,500).
- In a game with an engine copy the command is `node vendor/egret.mjs guide <question>`, and `node vendor/egret.mjs explain <ERROR_CODE>` gives an error's sentence in English and Chinese with its cards.
- One card: `--card <id>`. The cards of an area: `--list <area>`; of every area: `--list all`.
- All cards in one file: `--full --out <file>`.

`applies` names the engine entry or host. Stale cards say what changed.

Start a game with the cards `first-playable-world` and `choose-entry`; start on an error with `read-engine-errors`.

## Areas

- [game](areas/game.txt) · 6 cards · The first action, feedback in the moving body, saves, two languages and balance.
- [structure](areas/structure.txt) · 1 card · Rules apart from the picture.
- [entries](areas/entries.txt) · 2 cards · Which entry to start from, and the engine copy in a game.
- [input](areas/input.txt) · 11 cards · Touch, keys, gestures, phone screens, pause, UI callbacks, page buttons beside play and a turned table.
- [perf](areas/perf.txt) · 9 cards · Frame budgets, loop pacing, moving shapes, swarms, still backgrounds, resolution, 3D draw cost and shader stalls.
- [world3d](areas/world3d.txt) · 6 cards · Lights and shadows, cloth and rope, models in code, terrain, tile boards and toon figures.
- [render](areas/render.txt) · 6 cards · Many labels, wrapping, play's shapes, tints, changing pixels and a map under the camera.
- [animation](areas/animation.txt) · 1 card · Frame walks from an atlas and markers on a sequence controller.
- [physics](areas/physics.txt) · 1 card · Weights on rope joints in 2D, cutting them, and bodies that start held.
- [audio](areas/audio.txt) · 2 cards · Sounds made in code, mixed with cues and a limiter, and the clock of a rhythm game.
- [assets](areas/assets.txt) · 3 cards · Loading, startup and provenance.
- [ship](areas/ship.txt) · 4 cards · A web page, a phone app, a WeChat mini game or Steam.
- [verify](areas/verify.txt) · 1 card · From rule tests to device runs.
- [errors](areas/errors.txt) · 1 card · An engine error by its code.

## How cards are made

Cards come from games on the engine: `tools/harvest-games.mjs` proposes them, `tests/guide.test.mjs` checks them ([the loop](../knowledge-base/en/docs/agent-guide.md)).
