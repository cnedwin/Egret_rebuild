Make a survivors-like for a phone held upright. The player only steers a lantern bearer; a lamp, a bell and paper charms strike by themselves. Moths and shades come in growing waves for five minutes. Each level offers three upgrades to choose from. Win by lasting, lose when the light goes out.

Work in this folder. Read AGENTS.md first. When you are done, say what you ran and what you could not run.
