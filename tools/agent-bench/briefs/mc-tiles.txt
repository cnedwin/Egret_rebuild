Make a match-three board with a card battle above it, for a web page. Swapping two tiles that make a line of three or more clears them and charges energy; energy plays cards against one enemy, turn by turn. Three enemies in a row. Swap by drag, by two taps and by keys.

Work in this folder. Read AGENTS.md first. When you are done, say what you ran and what you could not run.

做一个网页游戏：上方是卡牌对战，下方是三消棋盘。交换两块后连成三块或以上就消除并充能；能量用来对一个敌人打出卡牌，回合制。连续三个敌人。可以拖动交换、点两下交换，也可以用键盘。

在这个文件夹里工作。先读 AGENTS.md。完成后说明你运行了什么、有什么没能运行。
