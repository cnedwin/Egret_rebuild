Make a short deduction game as a WeChat mini game. A merchant's ledger is gone. The player examines five pieces of evidence, takes words from them into notes, and fills the three blanks of a verdict: who, where and why. A wrong verdict says which blank does not hold. One case, Chinese and English.

Work in this folder. Read AGENTS.md first. When you are done, say what you ran and what you could not run.
